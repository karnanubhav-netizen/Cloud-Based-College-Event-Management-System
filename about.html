ABOUT SGC EVENTS

Where campus life
comes alive.

A cloud-based platform for discovering, registering and managing college events in one place.

THE PLATFORM

One place for every event.

SGC Events connects students and administrators through a simple digital event management experience.

Students can discover upcoming events, view event information, register online and keep track of their registrations.

Administrators can create events, upload event posters and gallery photos, monitor registrations and manage student participation.
